Queenstown, New Zealand Aurora Forecast
紐西蘭 · 皇后鎮南極光即時預報

Queenstown watches the Southern Lights (Aurora Australis) toward the southern horizon. Because geomagnetic latitude is 52°S, strong geomagnetic activity (G1–G2+ / Kp ≥ 5–6 or strong southward Bz) is needed — monitor NOAA solar wind and face south with an open lake view.
皇后鎮觀測的是南極光（Aurora Australis），地磁緯度約 52°S，通常需要 G1–G2 以上地磁暴（Kp ≥ 5–6 或強烈南向 Bz）才容易在南方低空出現，建議找南方無山遮蔽的湖岸。
