Why is raw NWP error ~25–33% here, while our 2-winter live app MAE is 18.3%?
(1) Raw Arctic NWP misses localized fjord clouds & marine stratus; our live app fuses real-time METAR/SMHI/FMI sky sensors within 55 km to cut `+1h` error from `24.0% → 18.3%` and False-Clear from `7.5% → 3.2%`. (2) Automated airport laser ceilometers (`NCD`/`CLR`) also cannot detect thin cirrus above ~12,500 ft, which inflates apparent error when high clouds are present.
為何這裡原始模型誤差約 25–33%，但兩冬實測 App 誤差僅 18.3%？ 因為上表是「未校正原始數值預報」的裸測基準；線上 App 實際運作時會融合 55 km 內氣象站雷射測雲儀，將假晴空率從 7.5% 壓低至 3.2%。此外自動測雲儀無法偵測高空薄卷雲，也會讓原始誤差看起來偏高。
